THE ORGANIZATION

What is Toastmasters?

Toastmasters International is a global educational organization that teaches public speaking and leadership skills through a worldwide network of clubs.

With 364K+ members across 16,200+ clubs in 145 countries, Toastmasters has a presence in corporate organizations, universities, and colleges around the world.
