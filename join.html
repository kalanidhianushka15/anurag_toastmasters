Ready When You Are

JUST
SHOW UP.

There's no application to join as a guest. Membership, if you want it after visiting, is a short separate step.

How it actually works

1
Come to a meeting as a guest
No sign-up, no fee. Check the Meetings page for the day, time, and venue.
2
Watch, or try Table Topics
You choose. Nobody's put on the spot without asking first.
3
Decide if you want to join
If it clicks, a committee member walks you through membership — cost, expectations, first role.
4
Start rotating through roles
Your first roles are usually small — Timer or Ah-Counter — before speaking roles.
