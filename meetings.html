What To Expect

ONE HOUR.
NO SCRIPT.

Here's exactly what happens if you walk into a meeting, minute by minute.

A typical meeting, roughly

0:00
Opening
Toastmaster of the Day welcomes everyone, runs through the agenda, introduces any guests in the room.
0:05
Prepared Speeches
2–3 members give short prepared talks — usually 5–7 minutes each, on a topic of their choice.
0:25
Table Topics
The impromptu segment. Random prompt, 1–2 minutes to answer, no prep time. Guests can try this too, if they want — never forced.
0:40
Evaluations
Each prepared speaker gets specific, structured feedback from an assigned evaluator — what worked, what to try next time.
0:55
Wrap-Up
Timer, Grammarian, and Ah-Counter give their quick reports. Meeting closes.
First Time Visiting?

Or

Either Way
